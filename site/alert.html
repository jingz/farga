Alert Component

Alerts provide contextual feedback messages for user actions and system status.

Alert Variants

Default: This is a standard neutral alert message.

Info: A new software update is available for your account.

Success: Your profile changes have been saved successfully.

Warning: Your subscription will expire in 3 days.

Danger: There was an error processing your payment request.


Example Code 
<div class="alert">
  <p><strong>Default:</strong> This is a standard neutral alert message.</p>
</div>

<div class="alert info" role="alert">
  <p><strong>Info:</strong> A new software update is available for your account.</p>
</div>

<div class="alert success" role="alert">
  <p><strong>Success:</strong> Your profile changes have been saved successfully.</p>
</div>

<div class="alert warning" role="alert">
  <p><strong>Warning:</strong> Your subscription will expire in 3 days.</p>
</div>

<div class="alert danger" role="alert">
  <p><strong>Danger:</strong> There was an error processing your payment request.</p>
</div>
